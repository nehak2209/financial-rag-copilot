INSTITUTIONAL REGULATORY COMPLIANCE MANUAL
SECTION 4: CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD) & ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) STANDARDS

1. CORPORATE VERIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
- Every commercial borrower must undergo automated screening against the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list and global PEP (Politically Exposed Persons) databases.
- Verification must confirm:
  1. Active status with state or national business registry.
  2. Matching legal corporate name and registered Tax Identification Number (EIN).
  3. Identified Beneficial Ownership (all individuals holding 25% or greater equity).

2. RED FLAGS AND IMMEDIATE INTERVENTIONS
- Any entity containing corporate naming conventions indicative of non-operating shell structures (e.g., words containing 'SHELL CORP', 'OFFSHORE NOMINEE', or unverified holding entities without physical premises) must be flagged with risk score > 90.
- Mandatory Action: Flagged entities cannot receive automated approval. The agent must halt the underwriting process and dispatch a manual suspicious activity report (SAR) evaluation to the AML Compliance Unit.
